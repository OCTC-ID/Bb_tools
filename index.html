Bb Tools

Small tools for building Blackboard Ultra courses at OCTC. Each one makes a PNG in OCTC navy and gold that you download and add to your course.

Course Banner Maker The banner at the top of your course. Add a title, an optional subtitle such as the term, and pick navy or gold.
